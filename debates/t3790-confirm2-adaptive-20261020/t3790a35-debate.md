We can stop regulatory capture if oversight bodies stay transparent and hire experts from universities instead of big tech labs. National safety agencies do not stop big labs from shipping risky code; they offer a legal shield by rubber stamping corporate papers as certified. If the US Department of Commerce hands safety rules to an unelected UN body, domestic firms trade public trust for global legal cover. Open source tech lives on not through safety agency waivers, but through the lack of licensing monopolies run by market leaders.
