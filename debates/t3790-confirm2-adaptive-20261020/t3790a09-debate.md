Demanding pre-deployment equity guarantees does not halt innovation; it sets a basic duty of care across the AI supply chain. Past failures of adjustment funds show that cash transfers fail to stop income inequality during major tech shifts. Federal regulators can enforce supply chain liability through laws that force employers to prove worker skill retention before deploying generative models. This matches OSHA rules that punish employers for chemical hazards before workers enter toxic areas.

Concentrating control within oversight bodies stops the permanent loss of human operational control in commercial services. Critics say that mandatory safety thresholds slow down deployment in global markets. However, speed is not a virtue when the failure mode involves permanent skill loss across millions of service workers.
