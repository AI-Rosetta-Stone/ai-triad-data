### Skeptic (skeptic)

Mandating third-party audits under current trade-secret rules remains legally contentious. Yet domestic manufacturing competitiveness survives only when oversight rules apply equally to all firms, regardless of size.

The Department of Commerce must stop using broad permission-to-build licenses. It must shift to narrow, verifiable runtime checks run by technically competent staff.
