The Federal Trade Commission and the Department of Justice can use antitrust laws to stop foreign evasion and domestic capture. This preserves a strict baseline of public accountability. Failing to set these infrastructure guardrails consigns society to unmanaged tech shocks with no rollback option once systems surpass human control.
